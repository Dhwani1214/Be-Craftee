the story so far

Hi, I'm the hands behind Be Craftee.

I picked up a crochet hook to fix a problem — I kept losing my page — and never really put it back down. What started as one uneven bookmark turned into a little shelf of yarn, a growing list of things I want to try, and a shop I run from the corner of my couch.

Everything here is made to order, in small batches, usually with a book open next to my hook. I like that it stays small — it means I can still make everything myself, check every stitch, and actually enjoy the process instead of racing through it.

  • HandmadeEvery single piece is made by hand, stitch by stitch — nothing here is mass produced.
  • Made to orderI make in small batches, so what you order is made specifically for you.
  • Slow, on purposeI'd rather take a bit longer and get the stitches right than rush a piece out.
